# Listing Policy

1. Review and classify material before public listing; raw intake never belongs here.
2. Publish only the minimum metadata needed for discovery, with a source link and provenance.
3. Separate facts, interpretation, proposal, and opinion. Label uncertainty.
4. Never expose private data, credentials, confidential discussions, security-sensitive details, or unlicensed full-text material.
5. Proposal states: draft, under-review, active, accepted, rejected, completed, superseded, withdrawn. Keep history after leaving active status.
6. Opportunity states: open, paused, filled, closed, cancelled.
7. Reference states: current, archived, disputed, unavailable.
8. Independent work states: listed, updated, withdrawn, unavailable. Do not relabel it official without a separate formal publication decision.
9. Listing is not endorsement, canon approval, official status, or rights clearance.
10. Correct errors transparently; retain change history and source provenance.
