# Rights & Attribution

- Link to authorized original publication pages wherever possible.
- Include creator/publisher attribution and license or permission information when known.
- Do not upload full third-party books, art, scripts, datasets, or other protected works without documented permission or a clear applicable license.
- A public URL does not itself grant republication rights.
- Distinguish fan/community works from official N.U releases.
- If rights status is unclear, list only a minimal factual pointer when appropriate, or do not list it; escalate for review.
- Requests for correction or removal should be recorded and handled promptly, preserving a private audit trail where appropriate.
